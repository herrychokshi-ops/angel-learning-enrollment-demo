Project overview

Angel Learning Center currently relies on paper and document-based enrollment forms. This proposal covers delivery of a branded online enrollment portal that lets parents complete the full enrollment packet digitally and lets staff review submissions in one place.

The interactive demo already prepared for your center is the foundation for the production build.

Scope of work

1. Parent portal & sign-in Secure parent access, account creation, and session handling
2. Guided enrollment packet All five forms in one checklist with progress tracking
3. Core enrollment forms Enrollment, tuition agreement, transportation, emergency medical, CACFP/IES
4. Staff review view Staff login to review received packets and submission status
5. Branding & bilingual UI Angel Learning Center branding with English / Spanish toggle
6. Hosting & handoff Deployed live site, walkthrough, and basic handoff documentation

Deliverables

  • Production-ready online enrollment web application branded for Angel Learning Center
  • Parent enrollment flow covering the five required forms listed above
  • Staff view for reviewing submitted enrollment packets
  • English and Spanish interface support
  • Live deployment URL and brief usage walkthrough for your team
